Meet Pip

This is Pip, a small fox with a very big curiosity. Pip climbs letters, tastes apples, flies kites, and asks a hundred questions. On every page, Pip is discovering something new — and so are you.

Can you find Pip on every letter?

How to read this book

Read it together. Look, say, and find Pip. There is no wrong way.
